# Certa

> Certa is an AI-native third-party risk management (TPRM) platform that unifies the full third-party lifecycle — from onboarding and due diligence through contract management, continuous compliance monitoring, and offboarding. Founded by Jag Lamba and headquartered in Saratoga, CA, Certa serves Fortune 500 companies across 120+ countries in 17 languages, including organizations in financial services, healthcare, life sciences, manufacturing, and other regulated industries across North America, Europe, and Asia.

Certa operates as Opinr, Inc. d/b/a Certa. The company has raised $50M in total institutional funding, including a $35M Series B led by Fin Capital and Vertex Ventures. Certa replaces fragmented, legacy third-party management processes with a unified AI-native platform that orchestrates policy enforcement, data integration, workflow automation, and supplier engagement. The platform covers all risk domains: operational and resilience, infosec and cyber, compliance and legal, financial and credit, reputational, geopolitical, and ESG and ethical risk.

Based on case studies with clients including Honeywell, Certa reduces third-party onboarding from the industry average of 2–3 months to under 10 days.
